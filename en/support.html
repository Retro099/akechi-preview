Service Flow

  1. 1. Inquiry: Contact us via official LINE or email, describing the test/questions you wish to analyze and current learning goals.
  2. 2. Submit Answer Data & Estimate: Send your answer records (question numbers, chosen answers, correct answers). We confirm scope, fees, and delivery timing.
  3. 3. Payment: After mutual agreement on scope, submit payment via our designated method.
  4. 4. Analysis & Report Generation: We thoroughly examine mistake patterns and grammar/reading friction points.
  5. 5. PDF Delivery: Your detailed PDF analysis report is delivered electronically.

Strict Privacy Handling: Submitted test answers and learning histories are handled strictly within the scope required to provide the learning support service. Answers and learning data are never published or shared in any personally identifiable format without explicit user consent.

Cancellation Policy: Cancellations prior to commencement of analysis work are accepted; refunds once analysis has begun follow production progress and applicable regulations. For details, please see our Terms of Service for Learning Materials.
