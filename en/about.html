About the Instructor

Akechi (Instructor)

After completing graduate studies in law school, I decided to dedicate myself to teaching Japanese to support international residents and professionals building their lives and careers in Japan.

I focus on careful 1-on-1 lessons online, primarily for test-takers preparing for JLPT N2 and N1, as well as professionals needing practical workplace and conversational Japanese. Instead of rigid curriculum, I tailor study plans, customized materials, and answer analysis to each student's specific goals and weak points.

Languages

  • Japanese: Native speaker
  • English: Capable of simple written communication and conceptual explanations

Teaching Philosophy

  • Individual Customization: Lesson pacing and plans are built around your current proficiency, upcoming exam dates, and available study hours.
  • Answer & Weakness Analysis: We look closely at mock exams and problem sets to understand why errors occurred and build concrete strategies to fix them.
  • Independent Study Support: In addition to live lesson time, you receive tailored PDFs and exercises designed to make self-study effective and structured.

Professional Scope & Important Legal Notice

"I hold no qualifications or registrations as an attorney (弁護士), administrative scrivener (行政書士), or tax accountant (税理士). When specialized assistance is required, qualified professionals will be introduced."

We do not draft or file legal or administrative documents, nor do we provide formal tax or legal counsel. Our services are strictly focused on language instruction and general educational information about living in Japan.
