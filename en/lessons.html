How to Get Started

  1. Contact Us: Reach out via official LINE or email.
  2. Goals & Level Check (Free 30-min Trial): Discuss your targets and experience a complimentary 30-minute trial session (once per person).
  3. Schedule & Prepayment: Agree on your lesson slot and course, followed by advance payment.
  4. Begin Lessons: Connect on Google Meet or Zoom according to your tailored plan.
