Payment Methods

Lessons are on a prepaid basis:

  • Students in Japan: Domestic Japanese bank transfer
  • Students overseas: Secure Stripe payment link

Direct bank wire transfers from abroad are not accepted due to unpredictable settlement timing. There is no automated checkout on this website; payment instructions are sent directly via LINE or email after your schedule is confirmed.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Overview

  • Up to 24 hours before lesson: Reschedule to another day OR 100% refund.
  • 24 hours to 3 hours before lesson: 50% refund OR reschedule to another day.
  • Under 3 hours before lesson: 0% refund, no rescheduling (100% cancellation charge).
  • Lateness: Arriving within 15 minutes counts as attended. Beyond 15 minutes is treated as an unexcused absence with no refund or rescheduling.

Please review the full terms on our Policies & Cancellation page.
