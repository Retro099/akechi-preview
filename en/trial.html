Steps to Your Free Trial

  1. Send a Message: Reach out via our official LINE or email with the checklist items below.
  2. Schedule Confirmation: Teacher Akechi will reply within 1 day to confirm a time that suits your schedule.
  3. Trial Lesson Day (30 Min): Connect over Google Meet (or Zoom) for an introduction, brief level assessment, and sample lesson.
  4. Custom Plan Review: After the trial, you will receive recommendations for your ongoing study plan (there is never any high-pressure sales pitch).

What to Include in Your Message

To help us prepare, please copy and include the following details in your message (written in simple English or Japanese):

  • 1. Your Name
  • 2. Current Japanese proficiency (and any current JLPT level held)
  • 3. Target exam and test date (if you are studying for a specific test)
  • 4. What you wish to focus on during lessons
  • 5. Preferred days and times for the trial (Weekends/Holidays between 10:00 and 20:00 JST)

Important Privacy Notice: Please do not submit photos of your Residence Card (在留カード), tax papers, or official visa application documents.

Trial Lesson Hours & Format

Platform: Online via Google Meet or Zoom.
