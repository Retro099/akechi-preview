Cancellation & Rescheduling

Notice Given Policy
Up to 24 hours before lesson Reschedule to another day OR 100% full refund
24 hours to 3 hours before lesson 50% refund OR reschedule to another day
Under 3 hours before lesson No refund, no rescheduling (100% cancellation fee applies)

Lateness Policy

Students arriving within 15 minutes of the start time are considered present, and the session will proceed for the remaining lesson time.

If a student does not join within 15 minutes of the scheduled start time, the session is recorded as an unexcused absence. In this event, the lesson is forfeited with no refund and no rescheduling.

Payment Guidelines

All lessons are offered on a prepaid basis:

  • Students in Japan: Domestic Japanese bank transfer
  • Students Overseas: Secure Stripe payment link (credit/debit card)

Direct bank wire transfers from abroad are not accepted due to unpredictable settlement timing. Payment links are delivered directly via LINE or email after your schedule is locked.
