Lessons & Instruction

Q. Can you guarantee that I will pass the JLPT?

We do not guarantee test passage. Success depends significantly upon your own dedicated daily study and retention outside lesson hours. We provide targeted instruction, meticulous answer analysis, and strategic preparation designed to give you the highest possible chance of passing.

Q. Are lessons conducted entirely in Japanese, or is English available?

Lessons are conducted primarily in Japanese to foster immersive learning. However, complex grammar points and nuances can be explained using simple English whenever necessary. Written messaging in simple English is also welcome.

Q. Where are lessons held? Are in-person lessons possible?

All lessons are conducted online via Google Meet or Zoom. We do not offer in-person lessons at this time. You can join from home or any quiet space with a stable internet connection.

Q. What days and hours are lessons offered?

Lessons are available on Saturdays, Sundays, and Japanese National Holidays between 10:00 and 20:00 JST (Japan Standard Time). Weekday lessons are not offered (weekdays are dedicated to lesson preparation and message replies).

Q. Are group lessons available?

Lessons are primarily 1-on-1 lessons. If you wish to take lessons with friends or coworkers as a small group, please contact us via LINE or email to discuss possibilities.

Q. Can absolute beginners enroll?

Yes. We offer starter courses covering Hiragana, Katakana, and basic conversational phrases, as well as an N5 basic Kanji course.

Payments & Cancellations

Q. What payment methods are supported?

All lessons are prepaid. Students residing in Japan pay via domestic Japanese bank transfer. Students overseas pay via secure Stripe online payment links. International bank wire transfers and cash are not accepted.

Q. Can I cancel or reschedule a lesson?

Yes. If you notify us at least 24 hours prior to the start time, you may reschedule or receive a 100% refund. Between 24 hours and 3 hours prior, you may reschedule or receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made less than 3 hours prior are non-refundable and cannot be rescheduled. See our Policies for full details.

Q. What happens if I am late to a lesson?

Arriving within 15 minutes of the start time counts as attended, and we will conduct the lesson for the remaining time. Arriving more than 15 minutes late is treated as an unexcused absence, and the session is forfeited without refund or rescheduling.

Study Materials & Scope of Assistance

Q. Can I share custom study materials with friends?

No. Custom PDF materials, problem sets, and weakness analysis sheets are strictly for the individual use of the enrolled student. Copying, redistributing, reselling, or uploading materials online is strictly prohibited.

Q. Can you help me prepare official visa paperwork or tax returns?

No. We do not draft government documents, assist with tax filing, or provide legal representation. "I hold no qualifications or registrations as an attorney (弁護士), administrative scrivener (行政書士), or tax accountant (税理士). When specialized assistance is required, qualified professionals will be introduced." Our focus is strictly on language education and general information about living in Japan.
